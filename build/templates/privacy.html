Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This is a draft starting point — please have it reviewed before relying on it as your official policy.

Who we are

the Knot and Narratives ("we", "us", "our") is a wedding and event photography and videography studio based in Agartala, Tripura, India, operated by Nilanjan Das. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you enquire through our contact form or WhatsApp, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, event date, and any details you share about your event.

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your booking
  • To plan and deliver photography/videography services you've booked
  • To send you your photo/video gallery after your event
  • To send occasional updates about your booking (never marketing spam)

Enquiry form & WhatsApp

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. When you submit an enquiry, your details are sent to Formspree's servers and then to our email inbox. You can review Formspree's own privacy policy at formspree.io. Messaging us on WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, as that conversation happens on WhatsApp's platform, not ours.

Photos and videos of clients

As a photography and videography studio, using select images and clips from real weddings and events in our portfolio, on this website, and on social media is part of how we showcase our work.

  • We only use images/videos from events we were booked to shoot.
  • We ask for your consent before publishing your photos or videos publicly — typically confirmed at the time of booking or delivery.
  • If you'd prefer your photos or videos are not used in our portfolio or on social media, let us know in writing and we will honour that request and remove any already-published content within a reasonable time.
  • Private client galleries delivered via Google Drive are never made public — access is only given to the couple/client via a private link or gallery code.

Client galleries (Google Drive)

Delivered photo and video galleries are hosted on Google Drive, shared either as a direct private link or via a gallery code on this site's Client Gallery page. This is a convenience delivery method, not a secure login system — please don't share your gallery code or link publicly. Google's own privacy policy governs how Google Drive itself handles data.

Data retention

We retain enquiry details and client contact information for as long as reasonably needed to provide our services and for basic business record-keeping. You can request that we delete your contact information at any time by reaching out to us directly.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to: tell you what information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, delete your information, or stop using your photos/videos in our marketing. Contact us using the details below to make any of these requests.
